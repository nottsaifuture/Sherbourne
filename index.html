THE SHERBOURNE

Make yourself at home.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Choose a floor to see inside, click a floor for room details, or click a door to open it.

In walkthrough, use WASD and the mouse. E operates the door you are looking at. Escape releases the mouse; Exit Walkthrough returns to presentation.

Room layout follows the supplied Keepmoat plan. Heights, undimensioned rooms, stair geometry and furniture are inferred. Furnishings and upgraded finishes are illustrative.

The supplied bathroom specification only includes an over-bath shower as standard on housetypes without an ensuite.
